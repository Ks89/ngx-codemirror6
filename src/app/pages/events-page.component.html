Events and methods

Outputs report editor lifecycle, document, update, and selection changes. Public methods are useful for actions triggered by your Angular UI, such as focusing, reading content, scrolling, or saving state.

  • Inputs configure the editor before users interact with it, for example [content] and [multipleSelections].
  • Outputs such as (contentChange), (update), and (selectionChange) notify your component when CodeMirror changes.
