Gutters and decorations

Gutters are columns beside the code, commonly used for line numbers, breakpoints, and folding controls. Decorations style document ranges without changing the text.

  • Inputs such as [breakpointGutter], [breakpoints], and [markedRanges] configure side columns and highlights.
  • Outputs include (breakpointsChange), emitted when the wrapper breakpoint gutter changes.
