Folding

Folding hides ranges such as function bodies when the active language can describe foldable syntax. Enable the fold state and optionally show a fold gutter next to the line numbers.

  • Inputs such as [codeFolding] and [foldGutter] enable folding extensions.
  • Config inputs such as [codeFoldingConfig] and [foldGutterConfig] customize the folded placeholder and the gutter markers.
  • Outputs are NOT emitted for folding state; read it with methods such as getFoldedRanges().
  • Template reference: #editor exposes public methods such as foldAll(), unfoldAll(), and toggleFold() to button handlers.
